# Cloudpine Coffee Roasters — Customer Support Guide

## About us

Cloudpine Coffee Roasters is a small-batch roastery in Da Lat, Vietnam,
founded in 2021. We roast in 5 kg batches twice a week, on Mondays and
Thursdays. Every order ships within 48 hours of roasting so the coffee arrives
as fresh as possible.

## Our coffee

| Coffee | Origin | Tasting notes | Price (250 g) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Langbiang Morning | Arabica from Cau Dat | orange, honey, black tea | $14 |
| Mountain Breeze | Arabica + Robusta from Lam Dong | chocolate, hazelnut | $11 |
| Highland Night | Dark-roast Robusta from Dak Lak | bold, burnt caramel | $9 |
| Tasting Box | 100 g of each of the three | — | $13 |

Every coffee comes as whole bean or ground. For ground coffee, choose the grind
for your brewer: phin filter, pour-over (V60), French press, or espresso.

Brewing recommendations:
- Phin filter or espresso: Highland Night or Mountain Breeze.
- Pour-over (V60): Langbiang Morning.
- French press: Mountain Breeze.

Coffee tastes best within 4 weeks of the roast date. Store it in the original
bag with the one-way valve, away from sunlight. Do not keep it in the fridge.

## Shipping

- Da Lat city: same-day delivery for orders placed before 2pm, $1.
- Nationwide: 2–4 business days via GHN, $2.
- Free shipping on orders over $40.
- Every order gets a tracking number in the format CPC-2026-xxxx, emailed as
  soon as the parcel is handed to the courier.
- We currently do not ship outside Vietnam.

## Returns and refunds

- Returns or refunds are accepted within 7 days of delivery if the product is
  damaged, the bag is torn, or you received the wrong item.
- Opened bags that are not defective cannot be returned, for food safety
  reasons.
- To request a return, email hello@cloudpinecoffee.com with your CPC order
  number and a photo of the product. We reply within 24 hours.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 3–5 business days.

## Payment

We accept bank transfer, MoMo, ZaloPay, Visa/Mastercard, and cash on delivery
(COD). COD orders over $40 are confirmed by phone before shipping.
